About me

Software that fits how people actually work.

I’m Asnaui Pangcatan, a full stack developer from Cebu. I started as an Android developer in 2016 and have since built web and mobile systems for organizations and small businesses — from internal tools for HR and operations teams to customer-facing booking sites.

Most of what I build replaces paper-based or manual processes: employee records and time tracking, performance evaluations, approval workflows, and online reservations with payment verification. I handle the full cycle — working out requirements with the people who will use the system, designing the data model, building and deploying, then supporting it in daily use.

  • Usable by real staff

    Interfaces shaped around daily users and refined through direct stakeholder feedback.

  • Secure by design

    Role-based access, input validation, and audit trails wherever records matter.

  • Maintainable

    Clear architecture that is straightforward to extend and support long term.

  • Built for the workflow

    Features scoped to the real process — from request to approval, or booking to confirmed payment.
